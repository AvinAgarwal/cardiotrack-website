Before you buy

Every question a lab asks, answered.

Is it a 3 channel or a 12 channel ECG?
A full 12 lead ECG, all leads recorded at the same time, diagnostic quality. It is not a single or 3 channel shortcut.
Is the AI and physician read included, or charged per report?
AI interpretation and physician sign off are part of the platform plan, so you are not billed per report. The plan covers the AI read, the physician signature, WhatsApp delivery and secure storage.
How many ECGs can I record?
There is no cap on recordings. You can screen as many patients as you like, the platform plan covers the interpretation and sign off.
Do I need a cardiologist to operate it?
No. Any trained technician attaches the leads and presses record. The AI and physician network handle interpretation. No specialist needed on site.
Do I need a printer for the signed report?
No. The signed report is a digital PDF sent on WhatsApp. You can also download and print it from the portal if you want a paper copy.
What is included in the kit?
The device, a full colour coded lead set, an Android tablet pre configured with the app, and onboarding support. The platform plan is billed separately from Rs 2,500 a month.
Are the leads reusable, and what do consumables cost?
The kit ships with a full colour coded lead set. Reusability, consumables and replacement pricing are shared with your quote, talk to our team and we will lay it out clearly.
What is the warranty?
Warranty period and cover are confirmed on your quote. Ask our team and we will share the exact terms before you decide.
Do you offer EMI, bulk or reseller pricing?
Yes. We have EMI, bulk and partner options for centres taking more than one unit. Tell our team your plan and we will price it.
What if my internet is slow?
The recording is stored on the device and uploads automatically when connectivity returns. Nothing is lost.
Is it validated and safe to use?
Yes. Cardiotrack is validated against international ECG standards and IEC 60601 tested for safety and performance, and is already deployed across India and 16 countries.
Can I see a demo first?
Yes. Message our team on WhatsApp and we will arrange a live demo at your location or on a video call.
